Publications & Research

Lingsen You (游凌森, also Lucas You), M.D., Ph.D., is an interventional cardiologist and researcher at Zhongshan Hospital, Fudan University. His work connects vascular biology, medical devices, flexible electronics, intravascular imaging, artificial intelligence and digital twins.

ProfileScientific profilePanvascular Radar

Research themes

Device–vessel suitcordance

A translational framework for matching an intervention with vessel anatomy, haemodynamics, biology and time—from preclinical validation to clinical use.

AI & digital twins

Using intravascular imaging, intelligent photonics and patient-specific modelling to move vascular care from static assessment toward adaptive decisions.

Panvascular platforms

Flexible electronics, intelligent fibres and organ-on-chip systems that connect sensing, intervention and longitudinal evaluation across vascular territories.
